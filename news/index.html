In the news

Odia newspapers, news sites and TV have covered the community events that girls, families and Youth Facilitators led in Ganjam. Tap any clipping to read it.

20pieces of coverage
5community events
2025 to 2026in print, online and on TV
  1. 11 Oct 2026

    International Day of the Girl Child

    Olapur village, Chikiti block

    Villagers, the Anganwadi worker and local women came together to talk about girls’ education, safety and rights. It is the event in Sabita’s story.

    1 piece of coverage: Matira Khabar.

  2. 12 Jun 2026

    World Day Against Child Labour

    Sheragada block

    Students from government schools across Sheragada block took part in a two-day drawing competition on child labour, with prizes given by the Block Development Officer. Around 80 people took part, including the Child Development Project Officer, teachers and Youth Facilitators.

    10 pieces of coverage: Dharitri, Ishwar, Kalinga Focus, Matira Jagruti, Prajatantra, Prameya, Rupalia News, Sambad and 2 more local outlets.

  3. 8 Mar 2026

    International Women’s Day

    Digapahandi block

    Women from self-help groups shared their experiences of empowerment, and local frontline workers were honoured for their service, with Youth Facilitators supporting the event.

    2 pieces of coverage: Chetana Media, Dharitri.

  4. 24 Jan 2026

    National Girl Child Day

    Tarinipalli village, Ganjam block

    A community discussion on the rights of the girl child, organised with Youth for Village and supported by the L’Oréal Fund for Women. Local officials, an ASHA worker and the Digdarshan Foundation joined the conversation.

    5 pieces of coverage: Dharitri, Manthana, Sambad, The Sakala, and on TV.

    Watch the TV report on Dig Darshan News
  5. 12 Jun 2025

    World Day Against Child Labour

    near Khallikot, Ganjam

    A community drama and discussion raised awareness of child labour. 78 local women took part.

    2 pieces of coverage: Pragativadi, Samaya.
